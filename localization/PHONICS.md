# English: adult-supported phonics practice

## Correction to the September 29 localization

The initial English release incorrectly reused a syllable-length/age-based
structure. Technical test success did not validate that teaching approach.
The English-only correction removes its 90-word syllable bank and age labels.
Other language pages are unchanged by this correction.

## Scope and sequence

This is supplementary practice for correspondences already taught by an adult,
not a complete phonics programme, automatic placement test or certified method.
The parent chooses a stage and a focus that matches the child's existing teaching.
Every focus lists the sound spellings needed; the adult models the sounds.

1. Short-vowel CVC words: short a, i, o, u, e, each practised separately.
2. Digraphs: sh, ch, ck, ng. Each focus uses three phonemes per word; the digraph
   is one visual unit with one sound line. Other letters each receive one dot.
3. Adjacent consonants: final n consonant groups, initial s/l/r groups, final st.
   These use four phonemes per word, with each consonant represented separately.

There are 14 authored focuses with six words each (84 unique words). Six is a
product choice for a short focused practice, not a research-established dosage.
Word order is authored within each focus. No random mixing of skills; only the
existing reward order is shuffled. Restart repeats the same focus.

Unintroduced long vowels, split digraphs, vowel teams, schwa, multisyllabic words,
exception words, q/qu and x are deliberately outside this version. These require
separate instruction and content design. No universal phoneme count is inferred
from letter count. All mappings are explicit for each included word.

## Interaction

- Sound clues → blend into the whole word → read aloud → self-confirm → chest.
- The whole word has no segmentation, hyphens or sound dots.
- Sound clues can be shown again without penalty.
- An adult checks the reading. The app does not listen, assess pronunciation,
  infer accuracy, award mastery or promote a child based on button clicks.
- Confirmation protects an active practice when changing focus or language.
- The final collection describes words practised, not words read correctly.
- There is no synthetic isolated-phoneme audio: letter-name TTS is not a
  substitute for verified phoneme modelling. Human-recorded audio and independent
  English literacy-specialist review remain needed for a standalone teaching mode.

## Methodological references (consulted 2026-09-29)

- [Department for Education: SSP criteria](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/phonics-teaching-materials-core-criteria-and-self-assessment/validation-of-systematic-synthetic-phonics-programmes-supporting-documentation):
  explicit cumulative correspondences, blending phonemes through the word,
  practice matched to known correspondences, and distinguishing individual
  consonants from digraphs. This app is not DfE validated.
- [University of Florida Literacy Institute: Foundations](https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/foundations/):
  explicit teaching, blending, practice with previously learned concepts and
  eventual connected text. UFLI materials or branding are not reproduced here,
  and UFLI has not reviewed or endorsed this app.

These sources inform the design principles, not approval of this exact word list
or evidence of learning effectiveness. The word lists are authored for this app.

## Verification

- Content tests: 84 unique words; 14 six-word focuses; legal explicit units;
  one short vowel per word; CVC / digraph / four-phoneme constraints; sh/ch/ck/ng
  integrity; no clustered st/sp/etc. as a single sound; no q/x shortcut.
- Every word exercised in Chromium, including whole-word reveal, repeat clues,
  completion, six rewards, double-click protection and focus switching.
- Keyboard Enter flow and cancellation of focus/language changes.
- Five viewport widths: 1440, 1024, 768, 390, 320. Existing other-language
  sessions tested for regressions and their generated files remain byte-identical.
- Visual review: desktop/mobile composition, visible word and action, sound
  marks and whole-word display. Technical checks are not pedagogical validation.
